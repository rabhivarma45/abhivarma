Famous attractions include the Sydney Opera House and Sydney Harbour Bridge. The city is also a major center for tourism and business.
10 The Taj Mahal is one of the most famous monuments in the world and a symbol of love. It is located in Agra, Uttar Pradesh, India, on the banks of the Yamuna River. It was built by Mughal Emperor Shah Jahan in memory of his wife Mumtaz Mahal and is famous for its beautiful white marble architecture.
